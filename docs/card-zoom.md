# Show a card larger

The floating cards in the 3D view are small, so that the house stays in sight. Since Home 3D 1.9.0 you can open one
near full screen, for example to read the solar forecast on a wall tablet or to use a TV's buttons on a phone.

## Open it

- **The ⤢ button** is on the solar card and on the cards of devices, plants, TVs and speakers, cars, racks and modules.
- **The solar card by touch:** the first tap grows the card. A second tap on the grown card opens it near full screen.
  The keyboard works the same way. With a mouse, the card grows while you point at it and a click folds it, as before.

Bin tags and "Today in the household" have no larger view.

The larger card shows everything, also when the small card was folded. The solar card shows its forecast and the extra
figures. The card's buttons and sliders work as usual. On a phone held upright, the card fills the width, and its curve
or the TV's artwork takes the height that is left.

## Close it

Use any of these:

- the close button (✕);
- a tap outside the card;
- **Escape**, or **Back** on a phone;
- the time you set for this device (see below).

The view also closes when its card goes away, for example when the cards are switched off.

## Two times for each device

Open **Settings → 3D view**. Both settings belong to the device you are on, and take seconds up to 3600.

| Setting | What it does | Empty |
|---|---|---|
| **Close a card shown larger after** | Closes the larger card after this time without a touch. Use it on a wall tablet. | It stays open until you close it. |
| **A touched card follows its rule again after** | A card that its [show rule](show-and-hide.md) would hide stays this long after your last touch on it. | 30 seconds. |

A card that is shown larger is never taken away by its rule. When you close it, the second time starts again.
